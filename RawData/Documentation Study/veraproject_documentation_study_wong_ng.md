**Journal:** *The Journal of Environmental Education*  
**Year:** 2026  
**DOI:** 10.1080/00958964.2026.2631991

## Purpose of the Study

The article is relevant to VeraProject because it examines how a **mobile plant identification app** can affect users' plant-related knowledge and encourage behavioral reflection in an environmental education setting. Since VeraProject is also focused on helping users understand plants and improve care habits, this study provides useful academic support for the project's purpose.

The article suggests that plant-related digital tools can support learning by making plant information more accessible, interactive, and connected to real-world observation. This supports VeraProject's goal of helping beginner plant owners move from confusion and guesswork toward clearer plant care understanding.

---

## Connection to VeraProject

VeraProject aims to help first-time plant parents and beginner collectors care for their plants through personalized information, care guides, and reminders. The study connects to VeraProject in three important ways:

1. **Knowledge Gains**  
   The article focuses on whether plant identification apps can help users learn more about plants. This connects directly to VeraProject's goal of providing crucial plant information in a beginner-friendly format.

2. **Behavioral Reflection**  
   The article also considers whether using a plant app encourages users to reflect on their relationship with plants and the environment. For VeraProject, this supports the idea that users should not only receive reminders, but also become more aware of their plant care habits.

3. **Digital Support for Plant Learning**  
   The study supports the idea that technology can make plant education more accessible. VeraProject can apply this by simplifying plant care instructions and organizing information in a way that reduces the need for users to search across multiple sources.

## Importance for the Problem Statement

The problem VeraProject addresses is that beginner plant owners often rely on scattered internet searches, complicated apps, or trial and error when caring for plants. This can make plant ownership frustrating and discouraging.

The article supports the need for a plant-focused digital tool because it shows that mobile plant applications can be used as educational resources. If users can identify, learn about, and reflect on plants through an app, then VeraProject can also help users build stronger understanding and confidence in plant care.

This supports the following VeraProject problem needs:

- Users need quick access to understandable plant information.
- Users need guidance that reduces confusion.
- Users need tools that help them form better plant care routines.
- Users benefit from digital systems that encourage learning and reflection.

## Design Implications for VeraProject

Based on the article's focus on plant knowledge and reflection, VeraProject should include features that do more than simply display plant care facts. The application should help users understand why each care task matters.

### Recommended Design Considerations

- **Use simple plant explanations:** Avoid overly technical plant care language when possible.
- **Connect care tasks to plant health:** For example, explain why overwatering can harm roots or why certain plants need indirect light.
- **Encourage reflection:** Include short prompts such as "How did your plant look after watering?" or "Did the soil feel dry before watering?"
- **Support learning over time:** Allow users to track plant care patterns so they can notice changes.
- **Make plant care visual:** Use icons, categories, or short summaries for watering, sunlight, humidity, and soil needs.
- **Keep the interface beginner-friendly:** The app should reduce stress, not create a new jungle of confusing menus.

## Application to VeraProject Features

The following VeraProject features are strengthened by this article:

### 1. Plant Profiles

Each plant profile should include clear care details such as watering frequency, sunlight needs, soil preferences, and general maintenance notes. This supports user learning by giving them reliable information in one place.

### 2. Personalized Care Guides

The app should guide users based on the plants they own rather than giving only general advice. This makes the experience more relevant and useful for beginner plant parents.

### 3. Care Reminders

Reminders should not only tell users what to do, but also help them understand the reason behind the care task. For example, a watering reminder could include a short note reminding users to check soil moisture first.

### 4. Reflection Prompts

A simple reflection feature could help users observe how their plants respond to care. This connects strongly to the article's focus on behavioral reflection.

Example prompts:

- "Did the soil feel dry before watering?"
- "Are the leaves looking healthy this week?"
- "Has the plant received enough light recently?"
- "Did you notice any yellowing, drooping, or new growth?"

### 5. Beginner-Friendly Interface

Since the project is intended for first-time plant owners, the design should avoid overwhelming users. Information should be organized into short, readable sections.

## Limitations and Considerations

While the article is useful for supporting VeraProject, the project team should be careful not to overstate its findings without reviewing the full text. The article focuses on a mobile plant identification app in an environmental education context, while VeraProject is a web application focused on plant care guidance.

Because of this difference, VeraProject can use the article as supporting evidence for the educational value of plant apps, but should still validate its own design with target users, such as first-time plant owners and beginner collectors.

Possible considerations:

- A plant identification app is not the same as a plant care management app.
- Environmental education users may have different goals from houseplant owners.
- VeraProject should focus on practical care routines, not only plant identification.
- User testing should confirm whether beginners find the app simple and helpful.
