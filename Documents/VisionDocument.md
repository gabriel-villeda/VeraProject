## Introduction

This document will describe our goals, scope, and high level requirements of the Vera Project. The purpose of this document is to provide a clear understanding of what the web application aims to achieve and to guide the development crew throughout the project. The application will provide a guide and aid to people aiming for better understanding of their plant providing in-depth information and guides for the optimal way of taking care of each user's specific plants. 

---

## Business Opprtunity 

Countless people who are first time plant buyers and beginner collectors tend to have a hard time keeping plants alive thanks to extensive research needed for each plant. The existing applications are often not user frinedly, and not very straighfroward, requiring more in depth knowledge on what plants usually need. 

---

## Problem Statement

Beginners usually rely on over-complicated apps and extensive research which is time consuming and often discourages first time plant parents.
First time plant parents need an application where they can:
- Obtain crucial information from plants
- Get a personalized guide for plants
- Recieve important reminders such as watering etc.

---

## Vision / Product Position Statement

For first-time plant parents, beginner collectors, and casual plant owners who want a simple way to care for their plants, **VeraProject** is a web application that provides personalized plant care guidance, plant information, and care reminders.

Unlike complicated plant care apps or scattered online research, VeraProject focuses on simplicity, clarity, and practical guidance. The system helps users understand their plants’ needs and stay consistent with care routines, making plant ownership less stressful and more enjoyable.

---

## Scope

The project includes the following functionalities:

- User account creation and login
- Ability for users to add and manage their plants
- Plant profiles with important care information
- Personalized care guides for each user’s plants
- Watering and maintenance reminders
- Basic plant care tracking
- A simple plant database or stored plant information system
- A user-friendly web interface
- High-level guidance for plant care needs such as:
  - Watering
  - Sunlight
  - Soil
  - Humidity
  - General maintenance

---

## Out of Scope

The following features are not included in the first version of VeraProject:

- Professional botanical diagnosis
- Guaranteed plant disease identification
- Integration with smart devices or plant sensors
- E-commerce features for buying plants or supplies
- Large-scale plant database with thousands of species
- Professional gardening or agricultural advice

---

## Key Features and Functionalities

- The application allows users to create an account and log in securely.
- The application allows users to add plants to their personal plant list.
- The application stores plant information in a database.
- The application provides care information for each plant, including watering, sunlight, soil, and general maintenance needs.
- The application allows users to view a personalized guide for their specific plants.
- The application allows users to track basic plant care tasks.
- The application sends or displays reminders for watering and other plant care activities.
- The application provides a simple and beginner-friendly interface.
- The application helps users stay organized by keeping plant care information in one place.
- The application is accessible through a web browser.

---

## Constraints

- The system will be developed by beginner-level developers.
- The project must operate within a limited budget.
- The application should avoid relying on paid external services.
- The first version of the system will have limited features.
- The system may not support a large number of users at the beginning.
- The plant care database may be limited to a smaller number of common plants.

---

## Assumptions

- Users have access to the internet and a web browser.
- Users have basic computer or smartphone literacy.
- Users are interested in learning how to care for their plants.
- The development team has basic knowledge of web development and databases.
- Users will manually enter their plant information.
- The first version will focus mainly on beginner plant owners.
- Plant care information will be based on general care guidelines.
- The project requirements may change during development.

---

## Risks

- Limited development experience may affect feature implementation.
- Time constraints may require reducing planned features.
- Plant care information may be too broad if the plant database is not carefully organized.
- Reminder functionality may be difficult depending on the technologies used.
- Users may expect advanced plant diagnosis features that are outside the project scope.
- Changes in project requirements may affect the development timeline.
- Database or server limitations may affect performance if the system grows.
- The team may need to simplify the design if certain features become too complex.

---

## Conclusion

VeraProject aims to make plant care easier and less overwhelming for beginner plant owners. By providing personalized care guidance, organized plant information, and helpful reminders, the application supports users in building better plant care habits.

The first version of VeraProject will focus on simplicity, usability, and practical plant care support. Future versions may include more advanced features, such as a larger plant database, improved reminders, plant health tracking, or AI-assisted plant recommendations.
